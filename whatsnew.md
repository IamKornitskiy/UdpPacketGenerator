## What's new in version 2.2.0

### Added
- **Theme support** — choose from four built-in themes to match your preference:
  - **Dark** — classic dark theme with high contrast
  - **Light** — clean light theme for bright environments
  - **Dark Blue** — dark theme with a blue accent
  - **Light Blue** — light theme with a soft blue tone
